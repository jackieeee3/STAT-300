Automobile manufacturer is exploring designs for water pumps for their off-road vehicles. They have been looking into four prototypes designed by two companies. Twelve pumps of each design have been randomly installed and tested in four-wheel-drive vehicles and the number of miles driven before the failure of water pumps were recorded.

The company is specifically interested in getting answers to below research questions

I. Whether or not the average mileage until failure is different in four brands

II. Designs 1 and 2 were presented by company A whereas Designs 3 and 4 by company B. Does the average mileage at failure differ by the company

III. Is the average mileage at failure different for the two designs, within each company?
